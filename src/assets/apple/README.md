The Apple mark for the client area's "Continue with Apple" button.

Unchanged images from Apple's official Sign in with Apple button service (logo style, black,
44 pt tall, at 2x and 3x):
https://appleid.cdn-apple.com/appleid/button/logo?height=44&color=black&border=false&locale=en_US&scale=2

The image is the white Apple mark on a black square. On the black button the square disappears
into the button, leaving the mark beside the button's own text, as Apple's guidelines for custom
buttons describe: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sign-in-with-apple
